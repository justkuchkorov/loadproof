🚚 LoadProof

Know your pallet survives the truck — before you stack it.
An AI plans the pallet · physics tests it at 0.5 g · a robot builds only what passed
Up to 25% of truck accidents are linked to badly secured cargo.
Stacking todayMixed boxes are still mostly stacked by hand.
Testing todayEU test EUMOS 40509: push the finished pallet at 0.5 g on a lab sled; it may shift at most 5% of its height.
The gapNobody tests the plan before the boxes are stacked.
European Commission, road safety: cargo securing · MMH, "Solving the mixed case pallet puzzle" · Lansmont: EUMOS 40509

How LoadProof works

📦 Orderany boxes — or a random one
→
🤖 AI plansGemini, 3 answers in parallel, best kept; lessons from past failures
→
✅ 3 checkswarehouse rules · stands · 0.5 g truck test in MuJoCo physics
→
🦾 Robot buildsmeasures every box, fixes what moved
✗ a failed check goes back to the AI with the numbers · nothing is built until the plan passes · a person can take over the hand with a webcam
